### Learning & Practice

* [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com) - The largest online chess platform, offering games, lessons, puzzles, and tournaments for players of all levels.
* [Lichess](https://lichess.org) - A free, open-source chess server with online play, analysis tools, studies, and training features.
* [ChessKid](https://www.chesskid.com) - A beginner-friendly platform designed specifically to teach chess to children through lessons and games.
* [Chessable](https://www.chessable.com) - Uses spaced repetition to help players efficiently learn openings, tactics, and endgames.
* [Internet Chess Club](https://www.chessclub.com) - A long-running online chess community featuring live play, coaching, and instructional content.

### Analysis, Study & Reference

* [Lichess Analysis Board](https://lichess.org/analysis) - Provides powerful engine analysis, opening exploration, and position study tools at no cost.
* [ChessBase](https://www.chessbase.com) - The industry-standard chess database and research platform used by many serious players and professionals.
* [365Chess](https://www.365chess.com) - Offers a large searchable database of games and opening lines for study and preparation.
* [FIDE](https://www.fide.com) - The official website of the international chess federation, featuring ratings, regulations, and major event information.
* [The Week in Chess (TWIC)](https://theweekinchess.com) - Publishes weekly collections of recent games, tournament reports, and downloadable databases used by chess enthusiasts worldwide.
